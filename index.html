03 · Conversion Surface

Landing Page Strategy

The landing page is the single most important conversion surface in the funnel. Its job is to translate a 3-second scroll on Instagram into a qualified inquiry.

  • PurposeConvert cold traffic into qualified leads who self-identify as ready to explore full-arch implants.
  • Trust BuildingDoctor authority, credentials, before/after imagery, real patient stories.
  • MessagingEmotional hook (life impact) paired with logical proof (process, expertise, outcome).
  • A/B TestingShort-form vs long-form layouts. Hook variations. Headline tone shifts.
  • Open Positioning QuestionPremium / concierge experience vs. accessible / approachable. The answer changes everything downstream.

08 · Honest Diagnostic

Where the Funnel Breaks

This is the section we most want your eyes on. We have a working system. We do not yet have a system that consistently produces the right patient at the right cost.

The Core Insight

The biggest problem is not lead volume — it is qualified leads.

  • AffordabilityMany leads cannot finance treatment at the price point required.
  • ReadinessSome are educated and intrigued, but not yet ready to commit.
  • Friction Drop-OffOthers fall out of the funnel due to fear, confusion, or process friction.
Open Questions for Jim
  • Where exactly are we losing qualified patients?
  • Are we attracting the wrong audience entirely?
  • Are we filtering too early — or not early enough?
  • Should we position more premium, or more accessible?
The Question Underneath All The Others

"How do we attract and convert the right patient — without adding too much friction?"
